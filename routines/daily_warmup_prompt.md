# Step 7 (Prompt 9) — Daily Warmup Routine Prompt

Schedule this prompt to run **once daily at `09:00 WAT` (UTC+1)** using Antigravity `/schedule` (which can invoke the browser subagent to browse naturally as your clip accounts).

---

```text
Every day around 09:00 WAT, open Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts in the browser
logged into our GTA 6 clipping accounts.

1. Spend 8 to 10 minutes on each app scrolling Reels, For You, and Shorts naturally.
2. Search GTA 6 terms ("gta 6 funny", "gta 6 npc", "gta 6 cops", "gta 6 lucia", "gta 6 jason").
3. Watch genuinely funny in-niche GTA 6 clips all the way to the end and like the good ones.
4. STRICTLY NO comments, NO DMs, NO follow-for-follow, and NO profile edits.
5. While inside each app, check what only the app UI shows:
   - Instagram: Settings -> Account Status (check Recommendations / Originality green status).
   - TikTok: TikTok Studio -> check for any "Unoriginal / Not eligible for For You" flags
     and note top viewer countries.
   - YouTube Studio: check Shorts "Viewed vs. Swiped Away" % and any copyright/reused notices.
6. Log what you did and every account-health reading in log.md (with date and WAT time).
   If any account status warning or unoriginal flag appears, alert the human immediately and
   do NOT change any profile settings without approval.
```
